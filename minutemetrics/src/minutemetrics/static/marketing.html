MinuteMetrics

Apple Health exercise-minute competitions for your own Home Assistant.

MinuteMetrics lets families and small groups compare Apple Health Exercise Minutes in monthly, yearly, or custom competitions hosted on a private Home Assistant instance.

Support Dashboard

Built For Friendly Motivation

Create multiple competitions, invite participants, and view standings, projections, recent trends, and daily movement from one dashboard.

Private By Design

Exercise-minute data is sent from paired iPhones to your MinuteMetrics server and stored in your own Home Assistant add-on database.

Simple Pairing

Admins create participants, scan a pairing QR code with the iOS app, and sync Apple Health Exercise Minutes to joined competitions.

Requirements

  • A Home Assistant instance running the MinuteMetrics add-on.
  • The MinuteMetrics iOS app installed on each participant's iPhone.
  • Apple Health permission for Exercise Minutes on each paired iPhone.
